# About This Website — Sarautile Ceramics

This is an online store for handmade ceramics. Below is a plain-English tour of everything it can do, for anyone (not just developers).

## For Shoppers

- **Browse products** — see all ceramic pieces, filter by collection or category, and sort them (e.g. price, newest).
- **Product pages** — each item has photos, details, and customer reviews.
- **Wishlist** — save items you like for later, even without buying yet.
- **Shopping cart** — add items, adjust quantities, and review before checkout.
- **Checkout & payment** — pay securely online (powered by Razorpay).
- **Account sign in / sign up** — create an account to track orders and reviews.
- **Leave reviews** — rate and review products you've bought.
- **"Notify me" for the kiln** — some pieces are made in batches (kiln runs); customers can sign up to be told when a sold-out or upcoming item is ready.
- **Wholesale inquiries** — businesses can submit a form to ask about buying in bulk.
- **Care guide page** — tips on how to look after ceramic pieces.
- **Our story page** — background on the brand/business.

## For the Store Owner (Admin Area)

A private dashboard, only visible to the shop owner/staff, to run the business:

- **Analytics** — see how the store is performing (sales, traffic, etc.).
- **Products** — add, edit, or remove items for sale.
- **Categories & Collections** — organize products into groups (e.g. "Mugs", "Summer Collection").
- **Inventory management** — track stock levels so items don't oversell.
- **Orders** — view and manage customer orders.
- **Customers** — see who's buying and their order history.
- **Discounts** — create sales, coupon codes, or special pricing.
- **Reviews** — moderate customer reviews.
- **Kiln sign-ups** — see the list of customers waiting for restocked items.
- **Wholesale requests** — view bulk-order inquiries from businesses.
- **Settings** — general store configuration.

## In Short

This app is a full online ceramics shop: a storefront where customers browse, save, review, and buy handmade pottery, plus a back-office dashboard where the owner manages products, stock, orders, discounts, and customer relationships — all in one place.
